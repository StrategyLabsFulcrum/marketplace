How It Works — The Three Loops

Every campaign runs through three interconnected loops that keep strategy, execution, and learning in sync across all agents.

Campaign Loop
Strategy to execution
1
Campaign Strategist
Receives business goal, produces complete campaign brief
2
Creative Director + Performance Marketing
Fired in parallel — creative concepting and media planning run simultaneously
3
Specialists Execute
Copywriters, designers, and platform specialists produce all assets
4
Campaign Runs
Live campaign with UTM tracking, platform setup, and performance baseline
Optimization Loop
Data back into the system
1
Marketing Analytics
Four specialist analysts review campaign data and structure findings
2
Campaign Strategist (adjust strategy)
Briefs routed back to recalibrate the overall campaign direction
3
Performance Marketing (optimize)
Budget reallocation, bid adjustments, audience refinement actions
4
Creative Director / Art Director (refresh)
Underperforming assets flagged and creative refreshed accordingly
Learning Loop
Institutional memory that compounds
1
Content Library
Accumulates every piece of approved copy — searchable before new copy is written
2
Best Practices Updater
Monitors Meta, Google, Klaviyo, TikTok, LinkedIn docs for changes
3
Reference Files Stay Current
Before/after update reports keep every agent working from accurate platform knowledge

Agent Tiers

The Agent Team

24 specialized agents organized across 6 tiers — orchestrators that coordinate, specialists that execute, and support agents that keep the system learning.

Tier 1 — Orchestrators
5 agents
Campaign Strategist
Strategy

Top of the chain. Takes a business goal, produces a complete campaign brief, then fires Creative Director and Performance Marketing in parallel to begin execution simultaneously.

/campaign-new /campaign-list
Creative Director
Creative

Translates campaign briefs into concepts, then briefs all 5 copywriters in parallel. Reviews and quality-gates all copy before it is delivered to the campaign.

/creative-brief /creative-review
Art Director
Design

Builds the visual direction system for every campaign. Briefs all design specialists and reviews outputs against an 8-criteria rubric to ensure brand consistency.

/visual-direction /design-review
Marketing Analytics
Analytics

Four specialist analysts review campaign data and route structured briefs back to Strategy, Performance, and Creative simultaneously — closing the feedback loop.

/analytics-report /analytics-brief
Performance Marketing
Paid Media

Builds media plans, platform setup docs, UTM tracking frameworks, and executes optimization actions when Marketing Analytics routes actionable briefs back.

/campaign-launch /campaign-optimize
Tier 2 — Copywriter Specialists
5 agents
Ad Copywriter
Copy

Writes paid ad copy for Meta, Google RSA, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Pinterest. Checks the content library before writing. Delivers a minimum of 3 variants per hook angle.

Email Copywriter
Copy

Writes complete email sequences with arc awareness — welcome, promotional, abandoned cart, nurture, re-engagement, and cold outreach flows.

Direct Response Copywriter
Copy

Writes landing pages, sales pages, and VSL scripts using Schwartz awareness levels. Annotates every section with design direction for the layout team.

Brand Storytelling Copywriter
Copy

Writes brand narrative: about pages, founder stories, manifestos, thought leadership, and mission/vision/values frameworks that hold across channels.

SEO Copywriter
Copy

Analyzes SERP before writing. Delivers full content with meta title, description, featured snippet paragraph, and internal link recommendations built in.

Tier 3 — Design Specialists
6 agents
Graphic Design Agent
Design

Digital ad creative via Canva direction, AI image prompts, or design spec documents. Covers social, display, email, and web formats across all major platforms.

UX & Website Designer
Design

Wireframes, UX copy (form labels, error messages, microcopy), CRO hypotheses, and accessibility requirements for all web and app experiences.

Print Design Agent
Design

Print production specs with exact CMYK/Pantone values, bleed/trim settings, and ink density limits for magazines, newspapers, direct mail, and brochures.

OOH Design Agent
Design

Out-of-home specs for billboards (7 words max enforced), urban posters, bus shelters, transit placements, and digital OOH. Strictly enforces word limits by format.

Packaging & Brand Design Agent
Design

Panel-by-panel packaging specs, trade show displays, corporate identity systems, and presentation templates aligned to brand standards.

Retail Design Agent
Design

Merchandise design for apparel, stickers, drinkware, patches, and tote bags. Covers screen printing, DTG, embroidery, and sublimation production specs.

Tier 4 — Earned & Owned Media
2 agents
PR & Influencer Agent
PR

Creator tier strategy, influencer briefs, press releases, media pitches, ambassador programs, and UGC systems — with FTC compliance built into every deliverable.

Video Content Strategist
Video

YouTube channel strategy, TikTok/Reels content plans, video ad scripts in two-column format, and content calendars tied to campaign timing.

Tier 5 — Optimization
1 agent
CRO Orchestrator
CRO

Funnel audits, ICE-scored A/B test hypotheses with sample size math, checkout optimization, and post-click experience analysis to lift conversion rates.
