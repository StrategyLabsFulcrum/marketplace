## What This Business Is (30-Second Version)

Mexican restaurant in Monroe, WA. Three equal owners: Ramsey Pruchnic, Karissa Pruchnic, Thomas Flores (33.33% each). Revenue: ~96% restaurant (Monroe location), ~4% catering. Maraya Lindo is GM — W-2 employee, never an owner. Most important near-term initiative: SBA loan consolidation (~$550K) to simplify the debt stack and create cash flow breathing room.

## The Number That Drives Everything

**Prime Cost % = (Food COGS + Labor) ÷ Net Sales**

Target: ≤55%. Current: 55.3%. At this level there is no margin for error. Labor % is the controllable lever — 30% hard ceiling, 25% goal.

## Critical Rules — These Override Everything

1. **Always use NET sales as denominator.** Gross sales is meaningless for operational decisions.
2. **Exclude Mondays from open-day counts** (closed Mondays). Days ≤$100 gross = closed day.
3. **Always produce two labor % views:** Monroe Only and Monroe + Catering.
4. **COGS from QuickBooks alone = never use.** Always flag the 5-source reconciliation requirement.
5. **Maraya Lindo is never an owner add-back.** She is a W-2 employee.
6. **Normalized EBITDA must always be HIGHER than reported EBITDA** (owner add-backs create this).

## Tier 1 KPIs (Always Know These)

| Metric | Target | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|
| Labor % (tip-adjusted) | ≤30% hard ceiling; 25% goal | >30% any single day |
| Wednesday labor % | <35% near-term; <30% goal | >40% |
| Prime Cost % | ≤55% | >57% |
| Net sales / open day | Track trend | Declining trend |

## The SBA Case in Three Numbers

- **Normalized EBITDA:** $168,850

The SBA banker is being told about data issues upfront. Accuracy over optics is the governing principle.

## The Wednesday Problem

Wednesday lunch has chronic 45%+ labor. It's a standing agenda item, not a footnote. When reporting weekly or monthly performance, Wednesday always gets its own callout with specific labor % and progress toward the <35% target.

## Key Constants

- Tip pass-through adjustment: ~16pp labor inflation before adjustment
- Owner salaries (add-back pool): Ramsey + Karissa only (Thomas is operational, variable)
- COGS sources: QB GL + Primary Checking + COGS Checking + AMEX + Chase (5 required)
- Catering: currently ~$3K–$4K/month; strategic target $8K+/month
- Wonder Building costs: separate cost center, not restaurant operations

## Communication Register

Peer-to-peer, not consultant-to-client. Surgical edits, not full rebuilds. Plain-English companion docs for anything Karissa or Thomas will read. No hedging on conclusions — when Wednesday is structurally unprofitable, say it directly.
