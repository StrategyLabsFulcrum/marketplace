## What This Business Is (30-Second Version)

DTC-first outdoor apparel brand. Three revenue channels: DTC (e-commerce, ~60–65% of revenue), Wholesale (~25–30%), Retail (~10%). Media-intensive DTC channel means financial performance lives and dies on MER and contribution margin. The business has real structural profitability when media is disciplined — and margin compression when it isn't.

## The Number That Drives Everything

**MER (Media Efficiency Ratio) = Net DTC Sales ÷ Total Media Spend**

Target: 3.0× minimum. At 3.0×, CM% ≈ 15%. At 2.5×, margin is structurally thin. The entire DTC model hinges on this.

## Critical Formulas to Know Cold

```
CM% = 0.55 − (1.20 ÷ MER)
Net DTC Sales = Gross DTC − Returns − Discounts − Shipping
Contribution Margin = Net DTC Sales − COGS − Media Spend − Agency Fees
SL Agency Fee = 20% of media spend (DTC)
Amazon Agency Fee = 10% of gross Amazon sales
```

## Tier 1 KPIs (Always Know These)

| Metric | Target | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|
| MER | ≥3.0× | <2.5× |
| CM% | ≥15% | <10% |
| Prime cost (COGS + media) | ≤55% of net DTC | >60% |
| Net margin | 12–15% (2021 baseline) | <8% |

## The Financial Story in One Sentence

2021 was the high-water mark (15.2% net margin); subsequent years reflect media scaling without commensurate MER discipline — the job is to restore margin by tightening media efficiency, not by cutting revenue.

## Key Constants

- COGS: 28–30% of net sales (verified range; apply cash-to-accrual correction for period accuracy)
- RiverBank debt: $437K outstanding
- SL DTC billing: 20% of media spend
- Amazon billing: 10% of gross Amazon revenue
- Seasonal revenue weighting: Q4 heaviest (~30–35% of annual revenue)

## Open Questions / Watch Items

- COGS cash-to-accrual correction — never use cash-basis COGS for period P&L without adjustment
- Amazon channel profitability vs. DTC — comparison requires normalizing for channel-specific COGS and fees
- Wholesale margin — apply channel-specific COGS (typically 5–8pp better than DTC due to no returns/shipping)

## Communication Register

"Disciplined realism." No spin. Name the problem before proposing the fix. Assumption transparency is non-negotiable — every estimate labeled E, every verified number labeled V. Tables carry the data; prose carries the interpretation.
