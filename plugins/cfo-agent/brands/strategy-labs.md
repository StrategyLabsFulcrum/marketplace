## The Number That Drives Everything

**EBITDA × Multiple = Valuation**

Active deal prices: $8M (VP equity deals) and $12.5M (Bryan Howell). Both require defensible EBITDA. Target 2026 EBITDA: $1.2M. At $1.2M and a 10× multiple, the $12M+ valuation is supported. The Griffiths appraisal at $4.197M is wrong and challengeable.

## The Single Biggest Differentiator

**95% recurring revenue.** This is what justifies SaaS-like multiples rather than traditional agency multiples. It must lead every banker, appraiser, and investor conversation. Never let it get buried.

## Critical Rules — These Override Everything

1. **QuickBooks "Services Previous Month" artifact** — QB revenue does not equal actual revenue. Always reconstruct from billings data; the "Services Previous Month" line is a QB timing artifact, not real revenue.
2. **IRC §174 normalization** — 2024 EBITDA was suppressed by capitalized R&D under §174. Always add back and explain when presenting to external parties.
3. **Normalized EBITDA > Reported EBITDA** — owner add-backs, §174 add-back, and one-time items all go in.
4. **Bad years get explained, not apologized for.** 2024 dip = §174 + WA DOR refund one-time event + timing. Always pair weak period data with the explanation and Q4 2025 rebound context.
5. **Griffiths appraisal is a starting point, not gospel.** Their weighted avg margin assumption (15.41%) is wrong. Q4 2025 actual margins: 28–30%. This is the core of the challenge.
