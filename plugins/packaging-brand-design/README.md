# Packaging and Brand Design Agent

Designs physical brand touchpoints — product packaging, trade show displays, corporate identity, signage, and presentation templates. Complete dieline specifications, CMYK/Pantone color, regulatory copy placement, and production documentation. Specialist agent — directed by the Art Director.

## What It Does

1. Loads brand standards (CMYK/Pantone, logo specs, identity system)
2. Reads the packaging/brand design brief from the Art Director
3. Produces complete design specifications for every physical brand asset
4. Handles regulatory copy placement for product packaging
5. Specifies production requirements per print method (offset, flexo, dye-sub, large-format)
6. Produces pre-production checklists and proofing requirements

## Asset Types Covered

| Type | Examples |
|------|---------|
| Primary packaging | Folding cartons, pouches, bottle labels, rigid containers |
| Secondary packaging | Outer cartons, display boxes |
| Trade show | Backdrops, pop-up displays, table throws, retractable banners, booth components |
| Corporate identity | Business cards, letterhead, envelopes, folders, email signatures |
| Signage | Exterior, interior, directional, wayfinding |
| Presentations | Keynote / PowerPoint / Google Slides master templates |
| Labels | Pressure-sensitive labels, shrink sleeves |

## Output

`campaigns/{{slug}}/creative/design/outputs/packaging-brand-design.md`

Complete design specifications with CMYK/Pantone colors, structural notes, regulatory copy placement, and production checklists.

## Dependencies

- **brand-intelligence-center** (required) — brand identity, CMYK/Pantone values, logo specs
- **art-director** (directs this agent) — visual direction system, packaging/brand brief
