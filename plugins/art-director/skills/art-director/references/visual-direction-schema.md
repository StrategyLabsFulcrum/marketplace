## 7. Channel Adaptation Rules

How the visual direction system flexes across different media without losing coherence.

| Channel | Adaptation | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| **Meta Feed (1:1, 4:5)** | Full system — primary reference format | Highest visibility, most design latitude |
| **Meta Stories/Reels (9:16)** | Vertical crop — subject in upper two-thirds, text in lower third. Motion preferred. | Immersive format, thumb-stop required in first 1s |
| **Google Display** | Simplified — logo + campaign line + single strong visual on brand color background. Minimal copy. | Small formats, fast scroll — clarity over complexity |
| **Email Header** | System on white background. Brand colors in accent elements. Campaign motif in header. | Email clients vary — keep it clean and scalable |
| **Organic Social** | More candid / less polished than paid ads. Same color/type treatment but more natural feel. | Organic content should feel native to feed |
| **Print (Magazine full page)** | Richest execution — full resolution photography, complete typographic system, campaign motif in full | Highest quality substrate, longest viewing time |
| **Print (Direct mail)** | Simplified for smaller format. CMYK. Campaign line dominant. One strong image. Clear CTA. | Viewed briefly — hierarchy and clarity critical |
| **Billboard (14'x48')** | Typography dominant. 7 words max. One image or strong color field. No body copy. | 3-second read at 65mph |
| **Transit (Bus shelter, subway)** | More copy than billboard (viewer dwells). Still type-dominant. Campaign motif prominent. | Captive audience, closer viewing distance |
| **Trade show (backdrop 10'x8')** | Environmental scale. Logo large and high. Campaign line. Brand color dominant. No small copy. | Read from 10–20 feet away in a busy environment |
| **Packaging** | Adapted for substrate and printing method. Brand standards primary, campaign motif secondary. | Permanent — not campaign-specific on primary packaging |
| **Deck / Presentation** | White background, brand colors for section dividers, consistent typography, minimal ornamentation | Projected and printed — must work at both scales |
