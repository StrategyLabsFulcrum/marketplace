# Influencer & PR Benchmarks

Reference data for evaluating creator performance, setting campaign expectations, and determining fair compensation. Update quarterly as platform conditions change.

## Engagement Rate Benchmarks by Tier

### Instagram

| Tier | Followers | Average ER | Good ER | Exceptional ER |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------|---------------|
| Nano | 1K–10K | 5–8% | 8–12% | 12%+ |
| Micro | 10K–100K | 3–5% | 5–8% | 8%+ |
| Mid-tier | 100K–500K | 1.5–3% | 3–5% | 5%+ |
| Macro | 500K–1M | 0.8–1.5% | 1.5–2.5% | 2.5%+ |
| Mega | 1M+ | 0.3–0.8% | 0.8–1.5% | 1.5%+ |

*Engagement Rate = (Likes + Comments + Saves) / Followers × 100*
*Note: Reels typically generate 2–3× the engagement of static posts for the same account.*

## Compensation Rate Benchmarks

Rates vary significantly by niche, audience quality, and brand/creator relationship. Use as starting negotiation ranges.

### Instagram — Sponsored Post (Static or Reel)

| Tier | Followers | Flat Rate Range |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Nano | 1K–10K | $50–$300 (often gifting only) |
| Micro | 10K–50K | $200–$1,500 |
| Micro | 50K–100K | $1,000–$3,000 |
| Mid-tier | 100K–250K | $2,500–$7,500 |
| Mid-tier | 250K–500K | $5,000–$15,000 |
| Macro | 500K–1M | $12,000–$30,000 |
| Mega | 1M+ | $30,000–$100,000+ |

*Rule of thumb: $10–$25 per 1,000 followers for nano/micro. Drops to $5–$15 per 1K for macro/mega.*
*Stories typically priced at 30–50% of a feed post rate.*

### TikTok — Sponsored Video

| Tier | Followers | Flat Rate Range |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Nano | 1K–10K | $25–$200 |
| Micro | 10K–100K | $200–$2,500 |
| Mid-tier | 100K–500K | $2,000–$10,000 |

| Mega | 1M+ | $20,000–$75,000+ |

### YouTube — Integration or Dedicated Video

| Format | Subscribers | Rate Range |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| Mention/integration (30–60 sec) | 100K–500K | $2,000–$8,000 |
| Dedicated video | 100K–500K | $5,000–$20,000 |
| Mention/integration | 500K–1M | $7,500–$20,000 |

### Usage Rights Premium

Base rate covers organic posting only. Additional usage rights cost more:
- Whitelist/paid amplification: +25–50% of base rate
- Usage in brand's own ads: +25–100% depending on exclusivity and duration
- Exclusivity (category, 90 days): +20–50%

## PR Benchmarks

### Average Pickup Rate by Outreach Type

| Outreach method | Average pickup rate |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Cold press release blast | 1–3% |
| Personalized pitch with story angle | 5–15% |
| Exclusive pitch to one outlet | 15–30% |
| Journalist with existing relationship | 25–50% |
| Product sample sent proactively | 10–20% (unboxing/review) |

### Earned Media Value (EMV) — Rough Estimates

EMV is a proxy metric for the advertising equivalent value of press coverage. Use cautiously — it is not actual revenue.

| Outlet type | EMV per mention / article |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| Tier 1 national publication | $20,000–$200,000+ |
| Category trade publication | $2,000–$15,000 |
| Niche blog with 50K+ monthly readers | $500–$5,000 |
| Podcast mention (50K+ downloads/episode) | $1,000–$10,000 |
| Local press | $200–$1,500 |

*EMV does not equal conversion value. A Vogue mention may not drive as many sales as a micro-influencer with a deeply aligned audience.*

## Platform-Specific Notes

### Instagram
- Reels have significantly higher reach than static posts. Budget for Reel deliverables.
- Stories are ephemeral; use for time-sensitive offers or product demonstrations with swipe-up.
- Carousel posts drive the highest save rates — use for educational or multi-feature content.

### TikTok
- Raw authenticity outperforms polished production. High-budget content can feel out of place.
- The hook (first 1–2 seconds) determines whether the video is served by the algorithm.
- Trends and sounds can amplify reach significantly — brief creators to use trending audio where appropriate.

### YouTube
- Dedicated videos drive higher purchase intent than integrations, but cost significantly more.
- Affiliate / promo code attribution works well on YouTube (viewers have time to act).
- Evergreen content continues driving value for months or years; factor this into rate negotiations.

## FTC Disclosure Requirements (US)

**Current requirements as of 2024–2025:**

1. **Clear and conspicuous disclosure** — must be in the content itself, not buried in #tags below the fold
2. **Instagram/TikTok/YouTube**: Use the platform's native "Paid Partnership" label; do NOT rely on #ad hidden among many hashtags
3. **Verbal disclosure in video**: Say "I'm partnering with [Brand]" or "This video is sponsored by [Brand]" in the first 30 seconds
4. **Gifted (non-paid) content**: If the creator received free product in exchange for a post, disclosure is still required — "#gifted" or "Thanks to [Brand] for sending this"
5. **Affiliate links**: Disclose affiliate relationships: "#affiliate" or "I earn a commission on purchases made through my link"

**Important**: FTC rules apply even to gifting-only relationships if there is any expectation of a post. Document all gifting programs and educate creators before sending product.

## Content Performance Benchmarks — Influencer Campaigns

Metrics to evaluate after a campaign:

| Metric | Below average | Average | Above average |
|--------|--------------|---------|---------------|
| View-through rate (video) | <15% | 15–35% | 35%+ |
| Link click rate (swipe-up / link in bio) | <0.3% | 0.5–1.5% | 2%+ |
| Conversion rate (visitors to purchase) | <1% | 1.5–3% | 3.5%+ |
| Promo code redemption rate | <2% | 3–6% | 7%+ |
| Earned media reach multiplier | <2× | 3–5× | 6×+ |

*Earned media reach multiplier = (total impressions from creator content) / (brand's paid media impressions for same budget)*
