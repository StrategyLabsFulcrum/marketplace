# Channel Framework

Reference for the Campaign Strategist. Defines channel selection logic, budget minimums, channel roles by campaign type, and allocation guidance.

---

## Channel Roles

Each channel has a primary role in the marketing funnel. Campaigns should assign channels to roles, not spread budget evenly.

| Channel | Funnel Stage | Primary Role | Best For |
|---------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Meta (FB/IG) | Awareness → Conversion | Prospecting + Retargeting | Visual products, impulse/emotional offers, broad consumer audiences |
| Google Search | Decision | High-intent capture | Branded, competitor, and problem-aware searches |
| Google Display | Awareness + Retargeting | Visual reach + follow-up | Retargeting site visitors, awareness at scale |
| YouTube | Awareness → Consideration | Story + demonstration | Complex products, trust building, before/after |
| TikTok | Awareness → Consideration | Organic-feeling discovery | Younger demos, entertainment-adjacent brands, product demos |
| LinkedIn | Awareness → Lead Gen | B2B prospecting | Professional audience, job-title targeting, high-ACV offers |
| Email | Consideration → Retention | Nurture + conversion | Owned list, lifecycle sequences, promotions, re-engagement |
| SMS | Conversion + Retention | High-urgency direct | Flash sales, appointment reminders, loyalty, cart abandonment |
| Organic Social | Awareness + Community | Brand building + engagement | Content distribution, community, social proof |
| SEO / Content | Awareness → Consideration | Long-term organic traffic | Problem-aware searches, evergreen content, thought leadership |
| PR / Influencer | Awareness + Trust | Earned reach + credibility | New market entry, brand launches, social proof at scale |
| Referral / Affiliate | Conversion | Trust-transfer acquisition | High-LTV products, strong community, word-of-mouth brands |

## Budget Minimums (Monthly)

Below these thresholds, channel performance is unreliable. Recommend consolidating to fewer channels rather than spreading thin.

| Channel | Minimum Effective Budget/Month | Why |
|---------|-------------------------------|-----|
| Meta Ads | $1,500 | Algorithm needs data to optimize; below this, learning phase never exits |
| Google Search | $2,000 | CPC competition makes low budgets unsustainable in most categories |
| Google Display | $1,000 | Low CPMs but needs volume to drive meaningful retargeting reach |
| YouTube | $2,500 | Pre-roll CPVs and production cost require scale |
| TikTok | $1,500 | Similar to Meta — algorithm-dependent |
| LinkedIn | $3,000 | High CPCs require scale to get statistically meaningful data |
| Email / SMS | $0 media (platform cost only) | Owned channel — no spend minimum |
| Influencer | $2,500 per engagement | One-off posts rarely move metrics; need volume or high-fit creator |
| PR | $1,500 retainer or $500/placement | Single press mentions rarely sustain |

## Channel Mix by Campaign Type

### Acquisition (New Customers)
**Priority channels**: Meta, Google Search, Email (new subscriber nurture)
**Supporting**: Retargeting (Google Display, Meta), Content/SEO for organic
**Avoid at low budget**: LinkedIn (high CPC), YouTube (production cost)

Recommended allocation:
| Channel | % of Paid Budget |
|---------|----------------|
| Meta (prospecting) | 40% |
| Meta (retargeting) | 15% |
| Google Search | 30% |
| Google Display (retargeting) | 15% |

---

### Launch (New Product / Brand)
**Priority channels**: Meta, Email, PR/Influencer, Organic Social
**Supporting**: Google Search (branded + category terms), YouTube if product needs demo
**Timing**: Awareness push 2–4 weeks before launch → Conversion-focused at launch

Recommended allocation:
| Channel | % of Paid Budget |
|---------|----------------|
| Meta (awareness + launch) | 35% |
| Google Search (branded + category) | 25% |
| PR / Influencer | 25% |
| YouTube / Video | 15% |

---

### Promotional (Sale / Seasonal Event)
**Priority channels**: Email, SMS, Meta, Google Search
**Key mechanic**: Urgency — limited time creates action
**Timing**: Teaser 5–7 days before → Hard launch → Peak (day before + event day) → Last chance

Recommended allocation:
| Channel | % of Paid Budget |
|---------|----------------|
| Meta (urgency creative) | 40% |
| Google Search (shopping + branded) | 30% |
| Google Display (retargeting) | 15% |
| YouTube (if video assets exist) | 15% |
*Email + SMS are owned — no paid budget allocation needed*

---

### Lead Generation (B2B or High-ACV)
**Priority channels**: Google Search, LinkedIn, Cold Email, Content/SEO
**Supporting**: Meta (retargeting), Webinar / event
**Avoid**: Broad awareness channels until qualified retargeting list is built

Recommended allocation (B2B):
| Channel | % of Paid Budget |
|---------|----------------|
| LinkedIn | 40% |
| Google Search | 35% |
| Meta (retargeting only) | 15% |
| Content / SEO | 10% (time investment) |

Recommended allocation (B2C lead gen):
| Channel | % of Paid Budget |
|---------|----------------|
| Meta | 45% |
| Google Search | 35% |
| Google Display (retargeting) | 20% |

---

### Retention (Existing Customers)
**Priority channels**: Email, SMS, Organic Social, Loyalty program
**Supporting**: Meta (custom audience — existing customers), Retargeting for cross-sell
**Avoid**: Prospecting spend — this campaign is for the existing customer base

Recommended allocation:
| Channel | % of Paid Budget (if any) |
|---------|--------------------------|
| Meta (existing customer custom audience) | 60% |
| Google Display (retargeting) | 40% |
*Primary execution is Email + SMS — no media spend needed*

---

### Winback (Lapsed Customers)
**Priority channels**: Email (winback sequence), SMS (if opted in), Meta (lapsed customer list)
**Key mechanic**: Personalization + incentive — "We miss you" + reason to return

Recommended allocation:
| Channel | % of Paid Budget (if any) |
|---------|--------------------------|
| Meta (lapsed customer list) | 70% |
| Google Display (retargeting) | 30% |
*Primary execution is Email sequence — no media spend needed*

---

### Brand / Awareness
**Priority channels**: Meta, YouTube, TikTok, PR/Influencer, Organic Social
**KPI shift**: Optimize for reach and CPM, not CPA
**Avoid**: Performance-only metrics — brand campaigns need longer measurement windows

Recommended allocation:
| Channel | % of Paid Budget |
|---------|----------------|
| Meta (reach campaign) | 35% |
| YouTube | 25% |
| TikTok | 20% |
| PR / Influencer | 20% |

---

### Competitive Response (Rapid)
**Priority channels**: Google Search (competitor + branded terms), Meta (conquest), SEO (competitor alternatives)
**Speed**: This campaign prioritizes fast activation over perfect creative
**Key mechanic**: Intercept competitor-aware customers at the point of search

Recommended allocation:
| Channel | % of Paid Budget |
|---------|----------------|
| Google Search (competitor keywords) | 45% |
| Meta (conquest targeting — competitor audiences) | 35% |
| Google Display (retargeting) | 20% |

## Budget Tier Guidance

### Under $2,500/month total
Focus on 1 paid channel only. Meta is usually the most flexible at this budget tier.
Always-on channels (Email, Organic Social) should carry the majority of the campaign load.

### $2,500 – $10,000/month
Can support 2 paid channels effectively. Recommended: Meta + Google Search.
Minimum $1,500 to any single paid channel.

### $10,000 – $25,000/month
3 paid channels viable. Recommended: Meta + Google Search + Retargeting layer.
Begin testing YouTube or LinkedIn if campaign type supports it.

### $25,000 – $50,000/month
4+ paid channels. Full prospecting + retargeting architecture. Begin influencer/PR.
Creative refresh cadence becomes critical — budget for creative production.

### $50,000+/month
Full channel mix based on campaign type. Channel-specific specialists warranted.
Attribution modeling becomes critical — data-driven attribution recommended.

## Creative Lead Time by Channel

Build this into campaign timeline planning:

| Channel | Creative Lead Time | Notes |
|---------|------------------|-------|
| Meta static | 3–5 days | Copywriting + design + review |
| Meta video | 7–14 days | Script + production + edit |
| Google Search | 2–3 days | Copy only |
| Email | 3–5 days | Copy + design + QA |
| SMS | 1–2 days | Copy + compliance check |
| Landing page | 5–10 days | Copy + design + dev + QA |
| YouTube pre-roll | 14–21 days | Script + production |
| Influencer content | 14–21 days | Brief + creation + approval |
| PR pitch | 5–7 days | Story development + media list |
