# /channel-strategy

Develop a full YouTube or TikTok channel strategy — positioning, content pillars, series architecture, SEO foundation, publishing cadence, and 90-day targets.

## How to Invoke

**Full YouTube channel strategy:**
```
/channel-strategy YouTube
```

**TikTok channel strategy:**
```
/channel-strategy TikTok
```

**Both platforms:**
```
/channel-strategy YouTube + TikTok
```

**Reposition an existing channel:**
```
/channel-strategy YouTube — reposition existing channel from [current focus] to [new focus]
```

## What You Get

- Channel positioning statement
- Audience definition (specific — who it IS and is NOT for)
- 3 content pillars with example video titles for each
- Series concepts (recurring formats)
- Publishing cadence recommendation
- SEO keyword foundation (YouTube: 10 seed terms; TikTok: hashtag and trend strategy)
- Thumbnail and visual style direction
- 90-day metric targets
- First 10 video recommendations (titles + hook concepts + type)

## Output Location

Saved to `brand-assets/video-strategy/channel-strategy-[platform].md`.
