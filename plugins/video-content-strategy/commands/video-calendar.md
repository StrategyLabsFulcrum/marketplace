# /video-calendar

Build a monthly or quarterly video content calendar across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and paid video.

## How to Invoke

**Monthly calendar from campaign context:**
```
/video-calendar campaigns/2026-03-spring-launch/brief.md
```

**Standalone monthly:**
```
/video-calendar April 2026 — TikTok + YouTube, product launch focus
```

**Quarterly:**
```
/video-calendar Q2 2026 — full video strategy calendar, all platforms
```

**Platform-specific:**
```
/video-calendar TikTok only, April 2026
/video-calendar YouTube only, Q2 2026
```

## What You Get

A structured publishing plan with one entry per video including:
- Working title and hook concept
- Platform and format
- Content type (evergreen / topical / brand / ad)
- Objective (awareness / consideration / conversion)
- Production complexity (talking head / B-roll required / demo / animation)
- Publish date
- Status tracker

## Output Location

Saved to `campaigns/[slug]/video-strategy/` or `brand-assets/video-strategy/` for channel-level calendars.
