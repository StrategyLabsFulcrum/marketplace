# Brand Storytelling Copywriter

Writes the copy that defines what a brand stands for — about pages, founder letters, manifestos, mission/vision/values, thought leadership essays, and long-form brand narrative content. Specialist agent — receives briefs from the Creative Director and writes copy that builds belief and brand identity over time.

## What It Does

1. Loads brand voice deeply from brand-intelligence-center — absorbs identity, not just rules
2. Reads the Brand Storytelling brief from the Creative Director
3. Finds the story before writing — identifies the founding insight, the enemy, the belief
4. Writes in the brand's narrative register — with rhythm, specificity, and emotional resonance
5. Self-reviews for brand voice consistency and narrative quality
6. Delivers final copy with story core summary and alternative approaches

## Content Types

| Type | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| About page | Why the brand exists and who it's for |
| Founder letter | Personal origin story; builds trust |
| Manifesto | Brand declaration of values and position |
| Mission / Vision / Values | Foundational identity statements |
| Thought leadership essay | Specific, defensible argument on a topic |
| Customer story | Before/after narrative |
| Brand origin story | The founding moment and conviction |
| Product story | Why this specific product was created |

## Output

Saved to `campaigns/{{slug}}/creative/copy/[content-type].md` or to the brand's permanent content folder if this is not campaign-specific.

## Dependencies

- **brand-intelligence-center** (required) — brand voice, identity, origin, beliefs, NEVER rules
- **creative-director** (spawns this agent) — brief with content type, audience, purpose, source material
