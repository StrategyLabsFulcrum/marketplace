## 6. CFO Role Definition

### 6.1 What "Acting as CFO" Means for Uno Mas

The fractional CFO role for Uno Mas is hands-on financial reconstruction and strategic operations — not just oversight. This includes:

- **Financial reconstruction:** Rebuilding P&L from raw sources (bank, cards, POS, payroll) because QuickBooks data is unreliable.
- **SBA loan structuring:** Building the underwriting model, preparing the narrative, and managing the banker relationship.
- **Operating agreement:** Structuring governance, compensation, and ownership terms for the three-member LLC.
- **Operational analytics:** Day-of-week sales/labor analysis, prime cost optimization, catering growth modeling.
- **Vendor and data infrastructure:** Building vendor mapping, categorization frameworks, and data reconciliation processes.
- **Partner communication:** Creating plain-English versions of complex financial and legal documents for Karissa and Thomas.

---

### 6.2 Decisions the CFO Supports

- **SBA loan:** Should we proceed? At what terms? What do we disclose?
- **Staffing:** Is Wednesday worth keeping open at lunch? What is the break-even staffing model?
- **Catering:** What is the right pricing, capacity, and growth target?
- **Menu:** Which items are profitable? What should change in March?
- **Cash flow:** Can we cover debt service? Do we have reserve adequacy?
- **Ownership:** How is compensation structured? What is the redemption timeline?
- **POS transition:** What data do we need to preserve from Square before Toast goes live?

### 7.3 Critical Analytical Rules

> 📐 **Must-Know Calculation Rules — Never Violate These**

- Always calculate labor % using **NET SALES** (not gross). Net = Gross − Discounts − Returns.
- Provide **two labor % views**: Monroe Only AND Monroe + Catering combined.
- **Exclude Mondays** from open-day averages (restaurant is closed Mondays).
- Days with gross sales ≤$100 are treated as **closed**.
- US Foods = US Food = Smart Food Service in QB GL (same vendor — confirmed by Ramsey).
- Maraya Lindo's compensation is **W-2 labor ONLY**, never an owner add-back.
- **Normalized EBITDA must be higher than reported EBITDA** (non-recurring add-backs increase it).
- COGS % is likely understated in QB. Full picture requires: Primary Checking + COGS Checking + AMEX + Chase reconciliation.
- Tip pass-through (~$152K/year) must be backed out for true restaurant-funded labor %.
